MoventraX

Product

One platform for EV rental companies

MoventraX is a multi-tenant rental operating system. The parent company runs the platform. Each customer company (tenant) runs its own riders, vehicles, hubs, plans and payments.

Riders

Sign in, complete KYC, take a plan, receive or book a vehicle, pay dues, report issues, and raise tickets from the phone.

Rental operators

Onboard companies, assign vehicles after KYC, track GPS, review documents, collect rent, and run workshops.

Platform operator

SuperAdmin manages organizations, branding, admin users, integrations and audit — without mixing tenant data.

Rules

  • Each rider, vehicle, booking, GPS point and payment belongs to one tenant.
  • SuperAdmin signs in without an organization and can switch companies.
  • Company users always work inside their tenant.
  • GPS and payment gateways are configured per tenant, not on the parent.
  • Tenant companies can be archived; parent and default sample tenant cannot be suspended.

Web · Riders & fleet

Onboard people and assets

Riders

  • Create and manage rider accounts, contact details and status.
  • Assign a rental plan and a vehicle after KYC is approved.
  • Open rider detail for KYC documents, wallet, dues and history.

KYC review

  • Queue of Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence and selfie.
  • View approved documents; approve or reject with a reason.
  • Vehicle assignment is gated until KYC is approved.

Vehicles & hubs

  • E-scooter, e-bike, e-rickshaw, e-car, e-truck inventory.
  • Hubs with geo location; charging stations and charger counts.
  • Install and activation dates on vehicles for commercial tracking.

Rental plans

  • Per-minute, hourly, daily, weekly and monthly products.
  • Charges, GST-aware billing, and plan changes for riders.

Web · Track & IoT

Live fleet control

Track Vehicle

  • Live map of all vehicles with battery and motion state.
  • History playback using GPS points with speed greater than zero.
  • Draw circular geofences and place points of interest.
  • Place a fence or POI by map click, vehicle click, search, or selected vehicle coordinates.
  • Six months of GPS history retained for investigation.

IoT & GPS integrations

  • Register devices (IMEI) and link them to vehicles.
  • Pull vendors such as Track360 / Traccar, or accept HTTP push.
  • WanWay and other TCP GPS protocols land on a dedicated telemetry host.
  • Commands: lock, unlock, ignition, location, battery, odometer, health.

Deployment topology

flowchart TB INET[Internet / TLS] INET --> ADM[admin web :3000] INET --> APIP[api :4000] INET --> TCP[telemetry TCP
21100 / 5001 / 8821] ADM --> APIP APIP --> MG[(Managed MongoDB VPC)] TEL[telemetry droplet] --> MG LOC[location :4100] --> SQLITE[(india.sqlite)] APIP --> LOC TCP --> TEL

Hosting split

  • App Platform: admin, API, location HTTP.
  • Managed MongoDB: riders, fleet, bookings, GPS history.
  • Droplet: WanWay / GT06 TCP ingest and telemetry config.
  • Location: reverse-geocode for India; used by API and maps.
  • No Redis / Postgres required for this product.
